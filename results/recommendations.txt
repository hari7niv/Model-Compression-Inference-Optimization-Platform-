=== Deployment Recommendations ===

Latency-critical (e.g. real-time / interactive serving):
  → fp16 (p50=4.95s, 19.9 tok/s)

Memory-constrained (e.g. edge devices, limited VRAM):
  → int4 (peak_memory=1313MB, perplexity=10.99)

Accuracy-critical, memory flexible:
  → fp16 (perplexity=9.89, lowest measured degradation)

Note: int8 did not lead in any single category in this benchmark run, but remains on the Pareto frontier — meaning it still represents a valid tradeoff point on at least one axis relative to every other config tested. Worth re-evaluating if benchmarking conditions or hardware change.
